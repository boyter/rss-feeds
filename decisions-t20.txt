# baohungyen.vn (Báo Hưng Yên điện tử, a Vietnamese provincial daily) carries a
# host rule written by an earlier queue pass:
#   region:asia topic:food topic:military topic:news type:publication

# the whole domain. It is right for two of the site's feeds and wrong for eleven:
# the life, travel, investment, heritage and reader-pages desks are neither about

# sections that DO have a subject get URL rules of their own, so the correction
# takes nothing away that was right.
# --allow-type-change is deliberate: this IS the decision, and rebuild --write
# reports every tag the old rule used to give.
